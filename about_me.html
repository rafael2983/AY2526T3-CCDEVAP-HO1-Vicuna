Who Am I?

I'm Rafael. At my core, I'm someone who's deeply interested in technology and how it continues to shape the world around us. Most of my time revolves around IT, but outside of that, I like to keep things balanced.

I enjoy going for jogs to clear my mind, playing guitar, mostly punk rock and alternative, and writing, which has become a way for me to think more clearly and express ideas that matter to me.

I try to stay optimistic in how I approach things, and I value being someone who can bring a bit of positivity and energy to the people around me.

You can find me at my happiest when I'm learning, creating, headbanging to my favorite music, or jogging in my spare time.
